Guarantee Frontier — Max Achievable Min Profit
Auto-computed on every Optimize run using fast iterations. The curve shows the optimizer's actual worst-case profit at each min-profit target. Where the curve leaves the diagonal (target = actual) is the maximum guarantee your budget and multipliers can support.
